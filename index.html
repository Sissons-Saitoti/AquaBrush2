Welcome to AquaBrush Kenya

We are dedicated to providing quality painting and plumbing supplies in Kenya.

At AquaBrush, we deliver dependable plumbing and painting solutions for homes and businesses. Our team is skilled in repairing leaks, unclogging drains, installing systems, and transforming spaces with professional painting. We focus on precision, efficiency, and lasting results. Every project is handled with clear communication and respect for your time and property. Whether it's fixing essential systems or refreshing interiors, we ensure the work is done right. Your satisfaction drives us — reliable service, quality finishes, and a team you can trust

Our Services

We offer a wide range of services to meet all your painting and plumbing needs.

1. Painting Services

- Interior & exterior house painting - Office, shop & commercial painting - Wall surface preparation & skimming - Ceiling, grille & door painting - Waterproofing & epoxy floor coating - Color consultation & spray painting - Pressure washing before painting

2. Plumbing Services

- Leak detection & pipe repairs - Bathroom and kitchen plumbing - Water tank, pump & pressure system installation - Drain cleaning & sewer unclogging - Geyser, shower & water heater installation - Pipe fitting: PPR, GI, PVC - Emergency plumbing - 24/7

3. Tools & Supplies Shop

  • Plumbing Tools: Pipe wrenches, pliers, threaders, PPR welding machines, plungers
  • Painting Tools: Brushes, rollers, spray guns, paint mixers, scaffolding, ladders
  • Power Tools: Drills, grinders, pressure washers, blowers
  • Materials: Pipes, fittings, faucets, sinks, paint, thinners, sealants, sandpaper
  • Delivery: Available across Eldoret and countrywide

4. Extra Services

  • Free site visit & quotation
  • Project management for construction finishing
  • Maintenance contracts for homes, schools & offices
  • Tool hire/rental for short-term projects
